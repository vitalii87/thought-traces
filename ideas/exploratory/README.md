# Exploratory Future Extensions

This directory preserves possible future continuations of the broader attractor framework.

The documents collected here are **not part of the Intelligence Attractor Hypothesis (IAH)**, its canonical claims, its supporting premises, or its present empirical program. They do not follow from IAH, are not required for IAH to be valid, and must not be used to reinterpret or protect IAH from negative evidence.

## Conceptual Relationship

The tentative research lineage is:

$$
\text{IAH}
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\text{possible broader adaptive generalization}
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\text{possible endogenous-constraint extension}.
$$

This is a lineage of questions, not a chain of established implications. In particular:

- IAH remains a domain-conditional hypothesis about independently optimized intelligent systems approaching a shared performance frontier;
- Constraint-Driven Adaptive Convergence (CDAC) asks whether a related pattern may occur beyond intelligence;
- Adaptive Dominance and Constraint Capture asks what may happen when an adaptive system begins to alter the optimization landscape itself.

These extensions remain outside the recommended empirical reading path unless they are later operationalized and supported independently.
